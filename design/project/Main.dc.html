SECURITY

Your traders' accounts stay theirs

Many bot sites ask traders to paste an API token, and that's how accounts get drained. Mwasya never asks for one.

Deriv OAuth only
Traders sign in on Deriv. We never see a password or permanent token.
Least privilege
Sites request Read and Trade only. They never get Payments or Admin.
Encrypted secrets
Telegram and affiliate tokens are encrypted on the server and never sent to the browser.
Checked bots
Every bot is checked against a list of allowed blocks before anyone can run it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Deriv account?+

Yes. You register your own app on Deriv's developer portal, and your traders log in with their own Deriv accounts.

How do I earn money?+

You set a markup of up to 3% on your Deriv app. Deriv pays it to you on every trade placed through your site. You can also add your affiliate token.

Can Mwasya access my traders' funds?+

No. Sites only request Read and Trade access, so neither you nor Mwasya can withdraw or move funds.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes, on the Pro and Agency plans. Add two DNS records and SSL is set up automatically.
